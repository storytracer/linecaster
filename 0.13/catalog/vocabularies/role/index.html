role¶

Vocabulary · closed scheme · classifies lines, regions

What a zone or range is, its content role, whatever layer a rendering puts it in.

Values¶

paragraph¶

Running text of the page's content, read as part of its main text.

Counts:

  • a paragraph of prose
  • a stanza or a block of verse
  • a block quotation
  • the text of a letter, a dialogue or a speech

Does not count:

  • a heading or a title (heading, title)
  • an item of a list (list_item)
  • a note keyed to a mark (footnote, endnote)
  • text in the margin beside the main text (marginalia)
  • text set apart in a sidebar or box (floating)

heading¶

A heading that names a section of the content that follows it, set on its own line or lines.

Counts:

  • a chapter, section or subsection heading
  • a numbered heading, such as "3.2 Methods"
  • a heading inside an article, a letter or a list of entries

Does not count:

  • the title of the document, an article or a chapter as a whole (title)
  • a table's column or row headings (table)
  • bold or spaced words that begin a paragraph (paragraph)
  • a running title in the top margin (header)

title¶

The title of a whole document, article, chapter or story, set as its main heading.

Counts:

  • a book's title on its title page
  • an article's title on its first page
  • a newspaper article's headline
  • a chapter's title where it opens the chapter

Does not count:

  • a heading of a section within the work (heading)
  • the work's title repeated as a running title in the margin (header)
  • the name of a periodical in its masthead's running line (header)

caption¶

Text that names or describes a table, figure, picture, chart or map, set next to it.

Counts:

  • a figure's or table's caption, such as "Table 2. ..." or "Fig. 3 ..."
  • the legend under a photograph or a plate

Does not count:

  • notes under a table, keyed to marks in it (footnote)
  • text drawn inside the figure (part of the picture, chart or map)
  • a line naming the photographer or rights holder (credit)

list_item¶

One entry of a list, with its bullet, number or letter where the zone holds it.

Counts:

  • an entry of a bulleted, numbered or lettered list
  • a step of a numbered procedure
  • an entry of a glossary or a list of names

Does not count:

  • an entry of a reference list or bibliography (reference)
  • an entry of a table of contents or an index (toc_entry)
  • a numbered heading (heading)
  • a row of a table (table)
  • a paragraph that merely begins with a number (paragraph)

list_label¶

The bullet, number or letter of a list item, set apart from the item's text in a zone of its own.

Counts:

  • a numeral or bullet a layout gives its own zone, left of the item's text

Does not count:

  • a label inside the zone of the item's text (list_item)

toc_entry¶

An entry of a table of contents or an index, naming a part of the work or a term, usually with where it is found.

Counts:

  • a line of a table of contents with its page number
  • an entry of an index with its page references
  • an entry of a list of figures or tables

Does not count:

  • an entry of a reference list (reference)
  • a heading of the table of contents itself, such as "Contents" (heading)

footnote¶

A note keyed to a mark in the text, set at the foot of the page or under the table it belongs to.

Counts:

  • a footnote with its mark
  • a note under a table keyed to a mark in it, such as "a P < 0.05"
  • a source line under a table

Does not count:

  • the part of a footnote carried over from an earlier page or column (footnote_continued)
  • a note gathered at the end of a chapter or book (endnote)
  • a running footer (footer)
  • a note in the margin (marginalia)

footnote_continued¶

The rest of a footnote begun on an earlier page or column, continued here without its mark.

Counts:

  • footnote text at the foot of a page that starts without a mark and finishes a note from before

Does not count:

  • a footnote that begins here with its mark (footnote)

endnote¶

A note keyed to a mark in the text, gathered with the others at the end of a chapter, article or book.

Counts:

  • an entry of a "Notes" section at the end of a chapter or book

Does not count:

  • a note at the foot of the page it belongs to (footnote)
  • an entry of a reference list (reference)

marginalia¶

A note in the margin beside the main text, printed or written there.

Counts:

  • a printed side note or shoulder note
  • a gloss beside the text
  • a reader's handwritten note in the margin

Does not count:

  • a running header or footer (header, footer)
  • a page number (page_number)
  • a column of the main text (paragraph)

drop_capital¶

An enlarged first letter of a paragraph or chapter, set into or beside several of its lines.

Counts:

  • a large initial that sinks into the first lines of the text
  • a decorated or historiated initial that begins the text

Does not count:

  • a capital letter in the size of the text (part of its line)
  • a large first word or line set on one line (part of the paragraph or title)

credit¶

A line that names who made or owns a piece of the content.

Counts:

  • a photograph's or illustration's credit, such as "Photo: ..."
  • an article's byline
  • a copyright or rights holder's line under a picture

Does not count:

  • a caption that describes the picture (caption)
  • a copyright line of the publication in the bottom margin (footer)

floating¶

Text set apart from the main flow of the page and placed where the layout had room for it.

Counts:

  • a sidebar or a boxed text
  • a pull quote
  • a short notice inserted between articles

Does not count:

  • a column of the main text (paragraph)
  • a note in the margin (marginalia)
  • an advertisement (advert)

reference¶

An entry of a reference list or bibliography, describing one work cited.

Counts:

  • an entry of a "References" or "Bibliography" section
  • a numbered entry of a list of works cited

Does not count:

  • a footnote or endnote that cites a work (footnote, endnote)
  • a citation within the text (part of its paragraph)

code¶

Source code, a command, a configuration or other text set as code, usually in a monospaced font or a code block.

Counts:

  • a program listing
  • a command line with its output
  • a configuration file's text

Does not count:

  • a name of a variable or a file in ordinary prose that is not set as code (part of its paragraph)
  • text that is typewritten (its production, not its role)

table¶

A table, a grid of cells in which a heading is shared by several cells, so that a cell is read by the column heading above it, the row label beside it, or both.

Counts:

  • a table with its header cells, body cells, row labels and section rows
  • a grid with no printed headings whose rows repeat the same columns, such as a price list or a data sheet

Does not count:

  • a form whose fields each have their own label (form)
  • pairs of labels and values side by side (key_value)
  • the table's caption or its notes (caption, footnote)
  • two columns of prose (paragraph)
  • a table of contents (toc_entry)

formula¶

Mathematical notation set apart from the text, as a display.

Counts:

  • a displayed equation, with its number
  • a system or derivation of equations set on several lines

Does not count:

  • a formula within a line of text (part of its paragraph)
  • chemical notation (chemistry)

chemistry¶

Chemical notation set apart from the text.

Counts:

  • a structural formula
  • a reaction scheme or a chemical equation set as a display

Does not count:

  • a chemical formula written in a line of text, such as H2O (part of its paragraph)
  • mathematical notation (formula)

music¶

Musical notation.

Counts:

  • staves with notes
  • tablature or another notation of music, with the words set under it

Does not count:

  • the words of a song set as text without notation (paragraph)

picture¶

An image on the page, drawn, printed or photographed, that is none of a chart, a map or an advertisement.

Counts:

  • a photograph, an engraving, a drawing, an illustration
  • an ornament, a vignette, a printer's device, a logo
  • a seal, a stamp or a signature taken as an image
  • a diagram drawn as a picture

Does not count:

  • a chart or plot of data (chart)
  • a map or plan (map)
  • an advertisement (advert)
  • a line or rule between parts of the page (separator)

chart¶

A chart or graph that shows data.

Counts:

  • a bar, line or pie chart
  • a scatter plot, a histogram

Does not count:

  • a table of the same data (table)
  • a drawing or diagram that shows no data (picture)
  • a map (map)

map¶

A map or plan of a place, showing its area, routes or features.

Counts:

  • a map of a country, a town or the sea
  • a plan of a site, a building or a battle

Does not count:

  • a chart of data (chart)
  • a picture of a landscape (picture)

advert¶

An advertisement, a notice set apart as one unit that promotes a product, a service, an event or a person.

Counts:

  • a display advertisement in a newspaper or magazine
  • a classified advertisement
  • a publisher's list of its other books at the end of a volume

Does not count:

  • an article about a product (paragraph, title)

form¶

A form, or a part of one, with fields to fill in or boxes to tick.

Counts:

  • a field's label with its blank, line, box or filled value
  • checkboxes with their labels
  • a questionnaire printed in a report

Does not count:

  • instructions on how to fill the form in (paragraph)
  • pairs of labels and values set side by side with no field to fill in (key_value)
  • a table read by its column headings (table)

key_value¶

Pairs of a label and its value set side by side, each label naming the value beside it.

Counts:

  • the lines of a data sheet, a receipt or a catalog record, such as "Date: 12 May"

Does not count:

  • a form's fields to fill in (form)
  • a table whose cells are read by shared headings (table)

header¶

A running header, text in the top margin that belongs to the publication rather than to the page's content.

Counts:

  • a running title, the work's or chapter's name in the top margin
  • a journal's name, an author's name or a date line in the top margin

Does not count:

  • the page number (page_number)
  • the article's own title on its first page (title)
  • a heading of the page's content (heading)

footer¶

A running footer, text in the bottom margin that belongs to the publication rather than to the page's content.

Counts:

  • a journal's citation line or a copyright line in the bottom margin
  • a running title set at the foot of the page

Does not count:

  • the page number (page_number)
  • a footnote (footnote)
  • a catchword or a signature mark (catch_word, signature_mark)
  • a table's last row near the bottom of the page (table)

page_number¶

The number of the page, printed or written on it.

Counts:

  • a page number in the top or bottom margin
  • a folio number written in the corner of a leaf

Does not count:

  • a number in the text or a table
  • a page number given in a table of contents or an index (toc_entry)

catch_word¶

The first word or syllable of the next page, printed at the foot of this one below its last line.

Counts:

  • a catchword in the bottom right corner of a page of an early printed book

Does not count:

  • the last word of the page's text (part of its line)
  • a signature mark (signature_mark)

signature_mark¶

A printer's mark at the foot of a page that numbers the sheets for folding and binding.

Counts:

  • a letter, a number or a symbol with a number, such as A2, B, 3*, at the foot of a page

Does not count:

  • a page number (page_number)
  • a catchword (catch_word)

separator¶

A line or rule that separates parts of the page and carries no text.

Counts:

  • a rule between columns or articles
  • a border or a frame around a part of the page

Does not count:

  • an ornament or a vignette (picture)
  • a dotted leader in a table of contents (part of its entry)

noise¶

Marks in the image that are not content of the page.

Counts:

  • specks, stains or dirt
  • a punch hole, a tear, the edge of the scanner, a finger
  • a color chart or ruler photographed with the page
  • bleed-through of the other side of the leaf

Does not count:

  • a stamp, a seal or a signature (picture)
  • a reader's handwritten note (marginalia)

other¶

Content that none of the other roles describes.

Counts:

  • content the annotator could not place in any other role

Does not count:

  • content that one of the other roles describes
