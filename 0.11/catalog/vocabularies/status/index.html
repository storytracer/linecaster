status¶

Vocabulary · closed scheme · classifies spans, words, lines, regions

Whether something on the page or in its source counts as the page's text, and why not.

Values¶

illegible¶

Text that is there but cannot be read, its characters too damaged, faded or obscured to make out.

Counts:

  • a ground truth's span marked unclear, scored as one U+FFFD per character
  • a word worn away on the page whose length can still be seen

Does not count:

  • text the source leaves out entirely (gap)
  • text that is legible but hard to read in this image (the property degraded)

gap¶

Text the source leaves out where the page has some; nothing is transcribed, and an empty span marks the place.

Counts:

  • an editor's omission, such as TEI's gap
  • a passage a ground truth did not transcribe, marked where it stands

Does not count:

  • text that is transcribed but cannot be read (illegible)
  • content the source keeps but leaves out of the text (suppressed, removed)

suppressed¶

Content on the page that the source records but itself keeps out of the page's text; it stays in the record and is rendered nowhere.

Counts:

  • a ground truth's zone it marks as not part of the transcription it counts, such as a library's label or shelf mark in the image

Does not count:

  • content a step or an importer takes out (removed)
  • a second copy of a zone (duplicate)

duplicate¶

A second copy of content another zone already holds, kept out of the text.

Counts:

  • a line detected twice, which the line assignment leaves out of the reading order

Does not count:

  • the same words printed twice on the page, such as a running title (each is the page's own text)

removed¶

Content a step or an importer took out of the page's text; it stays in the record, with its reason where an importer kept it, and is rendered nowhere.

Counts:

  • a page number typography removes as uncertain
  • text of a source's markup an importer keeps as a region with a reason (hidden, generated, editorial, ...)

Does not count:

  • content the source itself keeps out (suppressed)
  • a second copy of a zone (duplicate)

Reasons¶

Why something present in a source is no text of the page (a classification's reason).

hidden¶

A reader does not see it, and it takes no space on the page.

Counts:

  • markup a source's style hides, such as an element with display none

invisible¶

A reader does not see it, but it takes its space; a space or line break stays where it was.

Counts:

  • text a source's style makes transparent or white on white

generated¶

The source's software or markup draws it; the page does not print it.

Counts:

  • a number a wiki's template generates, a link icon, an automatic heading number the page lacks

editorial¶

The source's editors wrote it; the page does not print it.

Counts:

  • an editor's note, a header the edition adds, a correction note

Does not count:

  • an editor's correction of the page's own text (an edit, corrected)

error¶

A message of the source's software, not content.

Counts:

  • a template's or module's error message in the rendered markup

metadata¶

Markup that is not content of the page.

Counts:

  • style sheets, scripts, metadata elements

apparatus¶

The source's notation for something the page prints differently.

Counts:

  • a source's footnote mark where the page prints another mark or none

elsewhere¶

Printed text that belongs to another page or another place.

Counts:

  • a source's text of the next page's first line, carried over to this page's record

layout¶

Markup that only arranges the page and holds no text.

Counts:

  • an empty table row, a spacer
