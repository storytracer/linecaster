Reads a page of OmniDocBench's ground truth (one entry of its JSON list, as JSON) into a transcription (`import.omnidocbench.v1`, `linecaster.formats.omnidocbench.from_omnidocbench`): its elements as regions, their categories as content roles (the category kept as the region's label), their text as lines (the rows of their spans, a division mark the text dropped kept with a `joined` edit), inline formulas, tables from their HTML, display formulas from their LaTeX, the reading order of the elements that have one, captions, notes and continued paragraphs as links, its language as the page's classification and its other page attributes on the source; a ground truth by default (the option `ground_truth`), fitted to the page image where the entry states another size. `linecaster.formats.omnidocbench.entries` splits OmniDocBench's JSON into its pages for a table that `pipelines/import_transcriptions.py` reads with `--data-key`.
