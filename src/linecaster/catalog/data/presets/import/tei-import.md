Reads one page of a TEI P5 document into a transcription (`import.tei.v1`): a `sourceDoc` surface with its zones and lines, or a page of its text (`pb` begins one) with its `facsimile` zones or without geometry, with its choices as edits, unclear and missing text as statuses, `fw` as furniture, notes and `xml:lang`; the page by the option `page` (its number or the `xml:id` of its surface or `pb`, default the first), a ground truth by default (the option `ground_truth`), fitted to the page image where the file states another size.
