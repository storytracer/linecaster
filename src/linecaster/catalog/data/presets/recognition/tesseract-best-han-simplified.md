Tesseract 5 (LSTM, tesserocr 2.12.0 with Tesseract 5.5.3) with `script/HanS` (the model of simplified Chinese) from tessdata_best, on every line the line step found, cut out as its bounding box with 4 px white padding and the outside of its polygon painted white, 300 dpi assumed: page segmentation mode 7 (one line of text). The line's text is Tesseract's, in reading order; words, boxes and confidences are Tesseract's. CPU only, one line at a time. Tesseract's `preserve_interword_spaces` is set: without it Tesseract writes a space between letters it reads as words of their own (measured on the script samples, NOTES.md); this is the one setting that differs from `tesseract-best-latin`.
