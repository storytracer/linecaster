Character normalization for historic print in Latin script, the mapping table of the readable item texts: long s
to s, the ligatures (ﬀ, ﬁ, ﬂ, ﬃ, ﬄ, ﬅ, ﬆ) spelled out, r rotunda to r, a superscript e over a, o or u to the
umlaut. æ, œ, ø, å and ß stay, the text stays NFC (never NFKC) and spelling is never modernized. It fills
`Line.normalized` and `Paragraph.normalized`; the as-written texts are not touched.
